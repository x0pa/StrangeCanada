The best oddities in Canada aren't on any official list. They're the steel fish your uncle's town welded in 1987, the museum in a grain elevator, the landing pad nobody talks about anymore. If you know one, we want to hear about it.

**Email:** [hello@strangecanada.ca](mailto:hello@strangecanada.ca)

Write to us about:

- **A strange stop we haven't covered** — a giant statue, odd museum, mystery site, or ghost town, anywhere in Canada. Tell us where it is and what you know about it.
- **A correction** — a statue has moved, a museum has closed, a claim has a new challenger. Our Still Standing stamps are only as good as our latest check, and locals always know first.
- **Your photos and stories** — visited one of our stops on your own road trip? We love hearing how the trip went.

We read everything, and we reply to most of it — usually within a few days, a little slower in prime road-trip season.

**Please note:** Strange Canada doesn't book tours, sell tickets, or speak for any attraction. For hours, admission, and events, contact the attraction or town directly — and if their answer surprises you, tell us, so we can keep our pages honest.
